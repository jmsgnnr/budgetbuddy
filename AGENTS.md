## Signature

An indie pocket financial field guide, not an anonymous banking dashboard. Warm paper with a graph-paper dot grid, deep black type and panels, chartreuse progress accents and deliberate pink details. Heavy editorial display type + clean body + mono indexing. Clear six-month cash runway, respectful step-by-step questions and useful scenario sliders. Avoid corporate finance jargon, motivational clichés, meaningless $0 grids, blinding gradients and generic cards.

Visual changes require a design brief and desktop/mobile screenshots of blank and populated states. On phones show the primary numbers form early rather than hiding it behind a huge empty dashboard. Debt entry must remain optional and accessible. Keep creator credit, strong focus states, keyboard interaction and reduced motion. Never use em dashes in public text.

## Integrity

Local-first. No account, analytics, bank integrations or sending user-entered finances to servers. Never publish private plans, real budgets or stored browser data. The six-month reserve is a simple educational projection; explain the assumptions, deficit handling and that debt minimums are not automatically part of essentials. Preserve existing localStorage key to keep previously saved plans after the rename. Keep the 19 deterministic finance unit tests plus browser demo/math/deficit/privacy/mobile tests passing.

## Cost-aware delivery

Cursor for bounded implementation, deterministic changes for small fixes and Claude only for hard escalation. Do not publish unrelated repositories. Never treat successful arithmetic tests as proof of good product design. The lead must inspect actual pixels before publishing and verify GitHub Pages after pushing.
