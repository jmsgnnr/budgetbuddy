    The fine print

    How this plan works

    1. Start wherever you are. Having $0 saved is a valid starting point. No minimum amount is required to make a plan.
    2. Handle debt. Keep required payments current. If you have extra money, consider both a cash cushion and reducing expensive debt.
    3. Build a reserve over time. Six months of essentials is a reference goal, not an entry requirement or one-size-fits-all rule.

    Assumptions

    • Numbers are monthly and based on what you enter, not bank feeds.
    • Six months is a planning goal here, not a universal rule. Job stability and dependents change what you need.
    • Emergency money belongs in savings accounts, not the stock market.
    • Required debt minimums are not automatically included in the six-month reserve estimate. Add them to essentials if you want those payments covered.
    • This tool is educational planning, not personalized financial, tax, or legal advice.
